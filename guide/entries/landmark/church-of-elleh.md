## Overview

A roofless, ruined church a short ride from The First Step. Most players stop here first:
it has a grace, a merchant, and an anvil for upgrading weapons.

## Location

Northwest of The First Step, across the field where the Tree Sentinel patrols. Follow the
road north from the church to reach Gatefront Ruins.

## Sites of Grace

- Church of Elleh — inside the church, next to the anvil

## What's Here

- **Kalé** — merchant. Sells the Crafting Kit, cookbooks, a Torch, a Telescope, chain armor,
  arrows and bolts. See [Kale].
- **Smithing anvil** — upgrades weapons up to +3, for both regular weapons (Smithing
  Stones) and somber weapons (Somber Smithing Stones).
- **Renna** — gives you the Spirit Calling Bell and Lone Wolf Ashes, the start of Ranni's
  story. Once you have Torrent, she only appears if you **fast travel** to this grace from
  the map. Resting here, passing time, walking or riding back, or reloading the area
  won't make her show up.
- **Tree Sentinel** — patrols the road southeast of the church. See [Tree Sentinel].

## Items

Pickups:

- **Smithing Stone [1]** — right next to the anvil
- **Golden Rune [2]** — near the church's front entrance. Worth 400 runes when used.

Bought or given:

- **Crafting Kit** — bought from Kalé (300 runes)
- **Spirit Calling Bell** — from Renna, after you get Torrent and fast travel here
- **Lone Wolf Ashes** — from Renna, with the bell

Farmable (they respawn when you rest):

- **Erdleaf Flower**
- **Rowa Fruit**

## Tips

- Buy the Crafting Kit early: it unlocks crafting from the cookbooks you'll find. Short on
  runes? Use the Golden Rune [2] by the entrance, or sell it to Kalé.
- Grab the Smithing Stone by the anvil and upgrade your weapon to +1 right away.
- To meet Renna, fast travel here from the map after getting Torrent. Riding back won't
  work. Without her bell you can't use Spirit Ashes.
- The Tree Sentinel's patrol passes near the church. Ride around it until you're stronger.
