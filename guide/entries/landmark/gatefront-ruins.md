## Overview

A ruined camp held by Godrick's soldiers on the road to Stormveil. Clearing it gets you the
western Limgrave map, two underground cellars of loot, and an early Ash of War.

## Location

North of Church of Elleh along the main road. Stormgate and Stormhill are just beyond it to
the north.

## Sites of Grace

- Gatefront Ruins — at the edge of the ruins, by the road [check]

## What's Here

- A camp of **Godrick Soldiers** around a campfire, with an archer or two. See [Godrick Soldier].
- A **map stele** with the western Limgrave map fragment.
- Two **cellars** reached by stairs in the ruins, each with a chest. [check]
- Wagons with loot on the road through the camp.
- **Melina** can appear when you rest here and offer you Torrent, the spectral steed. She
  appears at one of the early graces (Church of Elleh, Gatefront, or Agheel Lake North). [check]

## Items

- **Map: Limgrave, West** — on the map stele by the ruins
- **Whetstone Knife** — chest in one of the cellars. Lets you apply Ashes of War at a grace.
- **Ash of War: Storm Stomp** — chest in the other cellar [check]
- **Flail** — on a wagon in the camp [check]
- **Lordsworn's Greatsword** — in a wagon on the road near the camp [check]

## Tips

- Get the Whetstone Knife here before anything else: without it you can't change Ashes of War.
- The soldiers fight as a group. Pull them one at a time with a thrown item or bow.
- Rest here a few times after exploring Limgrave if Melina hasn't appeared yet.
