## Overview

A ruined camp held by Godrick's soldiers on the road to Stormveil. Clearing it gets you the
western Limgrave map, the Whetstone Knife, an early Ash of War, and two weapons. Many
players meet Melina at the nearby Gatefront grace.

## Location

North of Church of Elleh along the main road. Stormgate and Stormhill are just beyond it to
the north.

## Sites of Grace

- Gatefront — by the large gate (Stormgate) just west of the ruins. There's no grace
  inside the ruins themselves.

## What's Here

- **Enemy layout:** several small groups of Godrick Soldiers spread through the ruins. One
  group has two wolves with it. A single Godrick Knight with a spear and greatshield
  patrols the main road. See [Godrick Soldier] and [Godrick Knight].
- **Map stele** with the western Limgrave map fragment.
- **One underground cellar,** reached by stairs in the ruins. It holds the chests with the
  Whetstone Knife and Ash of War: Storm Stomp.
- **Two wagons** with chests: the western one holds the Lordsworn's Greatsword, the eastern
  one the Flail.
- **Melina** often appears the first time you rest at the Gatefront grace and offers you
  Torrent, the spectral steed. She doesn't need you to find other graces first: resting
  at Gatefront (or Agheel Lake North) as your first open-world grace is enough, as long
  as you don't have Torrent yet. Some graces seem to be set up to bring her, others not.
  [check] which other graces trigger her

## Items

Pickups:

- **Map: Limgrave, West** — on the map stele by the ruins
- **Whetstone Knife** — chest in the cellar. Lets you change Ashes of War at any grace.
- **Ash of War: Storm Stomp** — chest in the cellar
- **Lordsworn's Greatsword** — chest in the wagon on the western edge of the ruins
- **Flail** — chest in the wagon on the eastern edge of the ruins
- **Ruin Fragment** (x3) — on a noble's corpse in a building in the southwest of the ruins

Farmable (they respawn when you rest):

- **Herba**
- **Rowa Fruit**
- **Erdleaf Flower**

## Tips

- Get the Whetstone Knife early. Without it you can only change Ashes of War at the
  blacksmith in the Roundtable Hold, once you can get there.
- Pick the soldier groups off one at a time. Most can be backstabbed if you sneak up on
  them.
- Watch for the Godrick Knight on the road. He hits harder and blocks with his greatshield.
- Resting at Gatefront is the quickest way to get Torrent: ride straight here from the
  Church of Elleh.
