## Overview

A merchant at the Church of Elleh, and the first merchant most players meet. He sells the
Crafting Kit and other basics.

## Location

Inside the Church of Elleh, next to the grace. He never moves.

## Questline

None of his own, but he's a step in Blaidd's. Once you've heard the howling in Mistwood,
ask him about it. He points you back to Mistwood, where you find Blaidd, and teaches you
the Finger Snap gesture.

## Rewards

- **Finger Snap** — gesture, taught when you ask him about the howling

## Tips

- Buy the **Crafting Kit** first. The **Torch** and **Telescope** are handy early on.
- **Cracked Pots** let you craft throwing pots, and you can only carry as many pots as you
  have Cracked Pots.
