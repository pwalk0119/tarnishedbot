## Overview

A masked man in white who mocks you when you first arrive in Limgrave. Only the start of
his quest happens in Limgrave. The rest takes place in Liurnia and beyond, and it ends
with an early route to Mohgwyn Palace.

## Location

Next to The First Step grace when you first enter Limgrave. He stays there until you've
claimed a Great Rune and spoken to Enia at the Roundtable Hold. After that he moves to the
Rose Church in Liurnia.

## Services

None while in Limgrave.

## Questline

In Limgrave:

1. Talk to him at The First Step. He sneers at you and tells you to follow the guidance of
   grace.
2. He stays at The First Step until you've claimed a Great Rune and talked to Enia at the
   Roundtable Hold. Then he leaves for the Rose Church.

Later steps (outside Limgrave, out of scope for this guide):

3. At the Rose Church he gives you Festering Bloody Fingers and asks you to invade other
   players three times. You can do this offline instead: use the red invasion sign at
   Writheblood Ruins in the North Altus Plateau to invade the NPC Magnus the Beast Claw.
   The step is done once Magnus is defeated or you've invaded his world three times.
4. He gives you the Lord of Blood's Favor and asks you to soak it in a maiden's blood.
   There are four sources:
   - the body at the Church of Inhibition
   - the body at the Chapel of Anticipation
   - killing Irina, near the entrance to the Weeping Peninsula
   - killing Hyetta, near the Lake-Facing Cliffs grace (this ends Hyetta's questline)
5. Bring the soaked favor back. He gives you the Pureblood Knight's Medal. Using it takes
   you to Mohgwyn Palace.
6. At Mohgwyn Palace you invade *his* world (he doesn't invade you). Defeating him there
   ends the quest.

## Rewards

- **Festering Bloody Finger** — given at the Rose Church
- **Lord of Blood's Favor** — given at the Rose Church
- **Pureblood Knight's Medal** — when you return the soaked favor
- **Varre's Bouquet** — for defeating him at Mohgwyn Palace (his final quest reward)
- **Festering Bloody Finger** (x6) — dropped if you kill him, along with 500 runes

## Tips

- There's no rush to talk to him: he doesn't leave The First Step until you've claimed a
  Great Rune and met Enia.
- Killing him ends his questline. You lose the Pureblood Knight's Medal route to Mohgwyn
  Palace.
- The medal is an early shortcut to Mohgwyn Palace, a popular rune-farming spot.
