## Overview

A masked man in white who mocks you when you first arrive in Limgrave. His quest is
mostly outside Limgrave and leads to an early route to Mohgwyn Palace.

## Location

Next to The First Step grace when you first enter Limgrave. He later moves to the Rose
Church in Liurnia. [check]

## Services

None while in Limgrave.

## Questline

1. Talk to him at The First Step. He tells you to seek the Two Fingers.
2. After you beat Margit, he's gone from The First Step and waits at the Rose Church in
   Liurnia. [check]
3. At the Rose Church he gives you Festering Bloody Fingers and asks you to invade other
   players three times. [check]
4. Then he gives you the Lord of Blood's Favor and asks you to soak it in maiden's blood at
   the Church of Inhibition. [check]
5. Bring it back. He gives you the Pureblood Knight's Medal, which takes you to Mohgwyn
   Palace. [check]
6. He invades you when you arrive at Mohgwyn Palace. Defeating him there gets you his
   weapon. [check]

## Rewards

- **Festering Bloody Finger** — given at the Rose Church
- **Pureblood Knight's Medal** — at the end of the quest
- **Varre's Bouquet** — dropped when you defeat him

## Tips

- Talk to him before you fight Margit, or he may leave before you've met him. [check]
- The medal is an early shortcut to Mohgwyn Palace, a popular rune-farming spot.
