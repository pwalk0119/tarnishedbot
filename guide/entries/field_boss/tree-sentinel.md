## Overview

A mounted golden knight that patrols the road near The First Step. He's well above
starting level and is meant to show new players that not every enemy must be fought right
away. Optional.

## Location

On the road between The First Step and the Church of Elleh, slowly riding back and forth.
He's the first enemy most players see.

## Stats

- **HP:** 2,889
- **Runes:** 3,200

## Strategy

- His halberd swings have long reach and delayed timing. Roll toward him and to his left
  (the horse's right side), not away.
- He blocks with his shield and can shield-bash you. Don't attack his front.
- Get behind the horse's flank and hit him there.
- It's far easier on Torrent: circle him and hit from the side.

## Rewards

- **Golden Halberd** — drop

## Tips

- Skip him at first. Come back after a few levels and a weapon upgrade.
- The Golden Halberd needs high Strength and Faith. [check]
