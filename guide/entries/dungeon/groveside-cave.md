## Overview

A short cave full of wolves. The boss, a Beastman of Farum Azula, drops the Flamedrake
Talisman, which cuts fire damage taken.

## Location

At the base of the cliff between main Limgrave and Stormhill, northwest of the Church of
Elleh. Stormfoot Catacombs is directly west of it.

## Sites of Grace

- Groveside Cave — right inside the entrance, next to a summoning pool

## Walkthrough

1. **Entrance.** The grace and a summoning pool for multiplayer are right inside.
2. **Wolf clearing.** The path opens onto a large clearing where you look down on a pack
   around a campfire: three Wolves and a stronger White Wolf. A withered corpse near them
   holds a Cracked Pot.
3. **Northwest end.** Two more Wolves wait further in, toward the northwest. One corpse
   holds Glowstone (x3), and another slumped against the back wall has a Golden Rune [1].
   Five Silver Fireflies sit on the ground to the right of that corpse. Four Cave Moss are
   spread around the chamber.
4. **North exit.** One more Wolf waits past the north exit. A short slope leads down past
   two more Cave Moss to the boss fog gate.
5. **Boss.** The Beastman attacks as soon as you enter. See Boss.

## Enemies

- **Wolf** x6 — 88 HP, 55 runes. They come in packs. Can drop Thin Beast Bones.
- **White Wolf** x1 — 466 HP, 97 runes. Leads the pack by the campfire.

## Boss

**Beastman of Farum Azula** — 708 HP, 1,000 runes, stance 65. Takes extra fire (+20%)
and slash (+10%) damage, and resists lightning (-20%). Many of its attacks can be parried,
and it can be backstabbed and stance-broken. It has several multi-hit combos with long
recovery afterward, so stay back, bait them out, and punish. Blocking drains a lot of
stamina, so dodge instead.

## Items

Pickups (one time only):

- **Cracked Pot** — corpse near the wolf pack
- **Glowstone** (x3) — corpse at the northwest end
- **Golden Rune [1]** — corpse against the back wall

Boss drop:

- **Flamedrake Talisman**

Farmable (respawns when you rest at a grace):

- **Silver Firefly** — 5, to the right of the Golden Rune corpse
- **Cave Moss** — 6: four in the wolf chamber, two on the slope to the boss

## Tips

- Wolves attack in groups. Fight them in a narrow spot so only one or two reach you.
- Pick up the Cracked Pot: each one raises how many crafted pots you can carry.
- Caves are very dark. Carry a torch or lantern.
