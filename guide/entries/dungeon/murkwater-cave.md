## Overview

A small bandit cave in the Murkwater river gorge. The boss is Patches, a Tarnished who
begs for mercy when he's losing. Sparing him starts his questline; killing him gets his
gear and Bell Bearing.

## Location

In the Murkwater river gorge, east of the Agheel Lake North grace and below it. Murkwater
Catacombs is further north up the same river.

## Sites of Grace

- Murkwater Cave — just inside the entrance

## Walkthrough

0. **Outside the entrance.** The NPC Bloody Finger Nerijus invades you here. This starts
   Yura's questline. See [Bloody Finger Nerijus].
1. **Grace.** The Murkwater Cave grace is just inside. There's a Cave Moss nearby.
2. **Bandit clearing.** Further down is a small clearing with two Highwaymen: one paces
   in the middle, the other sits by one of the exits. Bushes near the entrance let you
   crouch and hide, but three small chain tripwires are hidden in them. Touching one rings
   bells that call every bandit in the cave. A Cave Moss grows near the sitting bandit.
3. **Lower room.** The passage down leads to a small room with the rest of the bandits.
   A chest at the back holds Mushroom (x5), and there's a Cave Moss here. This room is a
   dead end.
4. **Hall to the boss.** Go back to the clearing and take the passage in its northeast
   corner. This short hall has two Cave Moss and ends at the fog gate.
5. **Boss room.** The arena looks empty apart from a chest. Opening it gives the Cloth
   Garb and Cloth Trousers, and then Patches drops down from a ledge behind you and
   attacks.
6. **Patches' surrender.** Below half health he stops fighting and begs for forgiveness.
   You decide whether to spare him (see Boss).

## Enemies

- **Highwayman** x6 — 188 HP, 59 runes, stance 30. Two kinds: dagger wielders and short
  sword wielders, and each can drop its weapon. One of the short sword bandits can also
  drop Mushroom or Smoldering Butterfly (20% each). Highwayman armor drops from all of
  them. They can be backstabbed, parried and stance-broken.

## Boss

**Patches** — 2,404 HP, stance 100, takes extra lightning damage (+25%). A Tarnished
like you, with a spear and greatshield. He can be parried and backstabbed, but not
stance-broken into a critical. Breaking his guard opens him to a riposte instead.

When he drops below half health he begs for mercy:

- **Spare him:** Golden Rune [1] (x2), the Grovel for Mercy gesture, and the start of his
  questline. He sets up shop in the cave. See [Patches].
- **Kill him:** 800 runes, Spear +7, Leather Armor, Leather Gloves, Leather Boots, and
  Patches' Bell Bearing.

## Items

Chests (one time only):

- **Mushroom** (x5) — at the back of the lower bandit room
- **Cloth Garb** and **Cloth Trousers** — the chest in Patches' arena
- **Glass Shard** (x3) — a trap chest that only appears after you spare Patches and he
  sets up shop. He hints that you should open it. It teleports you to Mistwood, where you
  have to fight or escape a Runebear.

Farmable (respawns when you rest at a grace):

- **Cave Moss** — 5 spots: by the grace, by the sitting bandit, in the lower room, and
  two in the hall to the boss

## Tips

- Caves are very dark. Carry a torch or lantern.
- Avoid the bushes near the entrance or step carefully: the tripwires bring every bandit
  at once.
- Patches' trap chest is a joke at your expense. Open it only if you're ready for a
  Runebear.
