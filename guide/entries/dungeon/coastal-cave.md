## Overview

A cave on Limgrave's western coast, packed with Demi-Humans. The boss fight is two
Demi-Human Chiefs, each with a group of followers. The exit past the boss comes out on
the island with the Church of Dragon Communion.

## Location

On Limgrave's western coast. A Nomadic Merchant camps a little south of the entrance,
under a large chunk of ruins.

## Sites of Grace

- Coastal Cave — inside the entrance, next to a summoning pool

## Walkthrough

1. **Grace.** The grace and a summoning pool are inside the entrance. Boc appears here
   once you've first met him by the Agheel Lake North grace. See [Boc the Seamster].
2. **Room 1, upper level.** Further in, a single Demi-Human stands straight ahead,
   looking over a ledge to the southwest. A Cave Moss is to his left.
3. **West path.** Don't drop down the ledge. Head west instead, along a path that slopes
   down. Two Demi-Humans (one with a scimitar, one with a club) wait by a Cave Moss.
4. **Room 1, lower level.** The path loops back into Room 1's lower level. Four
   Demi-Humans (two scimitars, two clubs) and a Large Demi-Human are here, with a Cave
   Moss near where the Large Demi-Human sits. A corpse by the south exit holds a Land
   Octopus Ovary.
5. **Boss.** The south exit leads straight into the boss room. See Boss.
6. **Exit to the surface.** Leave the arena to the southwest, past the return point. The
   path climbs back up past three Cave Moss, three Demi-Humans (one club, two scimitars),
   and a corpse with a Smoldering Butterfly. It comes out on the island off Limgrave's
   western coast, where the Church of Dragon Communion is.

## Enemies

- **Demi-Human** — 135 HP, 42 runes, stance 30. Scimitar and club variants. Can be
  backstabbed. Can drop String, Glass Shards, the Falchion or the Rickety Shield.
- **Large Demi-Human** x1 — 265 HP, 46 runes. In Room 1's lower level. Can drop the
  Spiked Club.

## Boss

**Demi-Human Chief** x2 — 609 HP each, 1,200 runes for the pair, stance 65. They take
extra damage from everything, especially slash (+40%). Both can be backstabbed, parried
and stance-broken. The first Chief has three scimitar Demi-Humans with him, the second
four club Demi-Humans. Stay near the arena entrance and the second Chief won't join right
away, so you aren't swarmed. Old Knight Istvan can be summoned to help. Once both Chiefs
are dead, any Demi-Humans still alive nearby stop being hostile.

## Items

Pickups (one time only):

- **Land Octopus Ovary** — corpse by Room 1's south exit
- **Smoldering Butterfly** — corpse on the path back to the surface

Boss drops:

- **Tailoring Tools**
- **Sewing Needle**

Farmable (respawns when you rest at a grace):

- **Silver Firefly** — 5, on the ground near the edge of the boss arena
- **Cave Moss** — 6: one in Room 1's upper level, one on the west path, one in the lower
  level, three on the path to the surface

## Tips

- Give the Sewing Needle to Boc at the Coastal Cave grace. This moves his questline on,
  and from then on he can alter garments for you.
- Fight the Chiefs one at a time by staying near the entrance.
- Caves are very dark. Carry a torch or lantern.
