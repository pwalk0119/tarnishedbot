## Overview

A short, beginner-friendly catacomb. The main hazards are flame traps on the walls. The boss,
an Erdtree Burial Watchdog, drops a Spirit Ash.

## Location

On the cliffs southwest of Stormgate, along the path up to Stormhill Shack. [check]

## Sites of Grace

- Stormfoot Catacombs — just inside the entrance

## Walkthrough

1. Head down from the grace. The corridors have flame-spitting wall traps on a timer: wait
   for each burst, then run past. [check]
2. Skeletons here get back up after you kill them. Finish them while they're on the ground,
   or use holy damage. [check]
3. Pull the lever to open the way to the boss room. [check]
4. Go through the fog wall to fight the Watchdog.

## Enemies

- Skeletons (they revive)
- Wall flame traps

## Boss

**Erdtree Burial Watchdog** — 1,200 HP, 1,300 runes. It lunges, slams its sword and later
sets its blade on fire. Stay close, dodge through the slams, and hit its sides. Spirit
Ashes help here.

## Items

- **Wandering Noble Ashes** — boss drop
- **Prattling Pate "Hello"** — in the catacombs [check]

## Tips

- A good first dungeon right after getting the Spirit Calling Bell.
- Go in at about level 10-15. [check]
