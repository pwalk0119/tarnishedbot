## Overview

A short, beginner-friendly catacomb and a good first dungeon. The hazards are fire traps
in the walls and Imps that wait in ambush and cause bleed. The boss, an Erdtree Burial
Watchdog, drops the Noble Sorcerer Ashes, and the Wandering Noble Ashes are a pickup inside.

## Location

Northwest of the Church of Elleh, at the base of the cliff that separates main Limgrave
from Stormhill, directly west of Groveside Cave. It isn't on the road up to Stormhill.

## Sites of Grace

- Stormfoot Catacombs — just inside the entrance

## Walkthrough

1. **Entrance.** Rest at the grace just inside, then take the stairs straight down. At the
   bottom a ghostly noble stands by the wall; you can talk to him. The hallway splits here:
   one way leads straight into a room, the other turns right.
2. **Room straight ahead.** Two Imps, one of them hiding in the left corner to ambush you.
   Two Root Resin grow here.
3. **Right-hand hallway.** It goes down some stairs to the room with the boss door. There's
   a Grave Glovewort [1] and a summoning pool here.
4. **Down the stairs to the left.** Two Imps wait on a ledge above this room. They either
   jump down to attack or throw darts from above. Pick up the Grave Violet and the Grave
   Glovewort [1].
5. **Narrow corridor.** Go straight through into the next chamber. One Imp clings high on
   the wall to your right as you enter, waiting to drop on you.
6. **First fire-trap corridor.** Wait for the trap to finish a burst, then sprint to the
   gap along the right side of the hallway. Don't go too far around the corner: a second
   trap covers the next hallway. The gap is a safe spot to wait in. From there, walk
   behind the first trap and hit it to switch it off (hitting it again turns it back on).
   The Prattling Pate "Hello" lies behind it.
7. **Second hallway.** Move along the right side again to another safe spot. It has a
   ladder going up (come back to it in step 9).
8. **Second fire trap and the Imp room.** From the safe spot, get into the trap's room and
   switch it off the same way. Behind it are five Imps: two hanging on the back wall, one on
   the left wall in the back corner, one crouched in the back-right corner, and one
   crouched by the corpse in the back-left corner. They only rush you together if you go
   for the corpse, so pull them one at a time first. The corpse holds Smoldering Butterfly
   (x3), with a Ghost Glovewort [1] right next to it.
9. **Up the ladder.** It leads to the top level of the room from step 4, where the Imps
   were on the ledge. One Imp is right to your left as you arrive and another guards the
   exit. Both are crouched, so you can get the drop on at least one. A Grave Glovewort [1]
   sits near the ledge.
10. **Dart Imp's room.** Go straight through the corridor. An Imp appears in the next room
    and starts throwing darts. This room is a dead end, but it holds the **Wandering Noble
    Ashes** on a noble's corpse, with the fourth Grave Glovewort [1] next to it.
11. **Lever.** Go back to the ledge room and drop down from the ledge to its middle level.
    Pull the lever there: it opens the boss door.
12. **Boss.** Return to the room from step 3 and go through the boss door to fight the
    Erdtree Burial Watchdog. Use the summoning pool, the Wandering Noble Ashes, or another
    Spirit Ash.

## Enemies

- **Imps** — small, fast, and hide in ambush spots: in corners, on ledges and high on
  walls. Their attacks build up bleed, and some throw darts. 196 HP and 67 runes each in
  Limgrave. They can be backstabbed. See [Imp].
- **Fire traps** — statues in the walls that spray fire on a timer. Hit one from behind to
  switch it off.

## Boss

**Erdtree Burial Watchdog** — 1,200 HP, 1,300 runes. It lunges, slams its sword and later
sets its blade on fire. Stay close, dodge through the slams, and hit its sides. It can't be
backstabbed, but its stance can be broken for a critical hit. Spirit Ashes help here.

## Items

Pickups (one time only):

- **Wandering Noble Ashes** — on a noble's corpse in the dart Imp's room (dead end)
- **Grave Glovewort [1]** — four separate pickups: in the boss-door room, in the ledge
  room (lower and upper level), and next to the Wandering Noble Ashes
- **Ghost Glovewort [1]** — next to the corpse in the five-Imp room
- **Prattling Pate "Hello"** — behind the first fire trap
- **Smoldering Butterfly** (x3) — on the corpse in the five-Imp room

Boss drop:

- **Noble Sorcerer Ashes** — from the Erdtree Burial Watchdog

Farmable (they respawn when you rest):

- **Root Resin** — two in the room straight ahead from the entrance
- **Grave Violet**
- **Smoldering Butterfly**

## Tips

- A good first dungeon right after getting the Spirit Calling Bell.
- Go in at about level 10-15.
- Imp attacks cause bleed. Block with a shield and use guard counters to punish them, and
  back off before the bleed meter fills. An Imp's stance is low (30): a single guard
  counter from a straight sword breaks it and opens it to a critical hit.
- Switch off fire traps from behind instead of running past them repeatedly.
- Grave and Ghost Glovewort upgrade Spirit Ashes, so pick up every one you see.
