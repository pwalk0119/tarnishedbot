## Overview

A small catacomb full of Imps, with fire-arrow traps triggered by pressure plates. A lever
opens the boss door. The boss, a Grave Warden Duelist, drops the Banished Knight Engvall
spirit ashes.

## Location

North up the Murkwater river from Murkwater Cave. The Murkwater Coast grace sits on a
short cliff above the river; from there, use the spiritspring to the north to drop down to
the entrance.

## Sites of Grace

- Murkwater Catacombs — inside the entrance

## Walkthrough

1. **Room 1.** Past the grace is the first room, with three Imps: one crouched in the
   east corner, one hanging on the southwest wall above the southwest exit, and one
   hanging from the ceiling in the middle. Two Grave Glovewort [1] are in this room.
2. **Split hall.** Take the southwest exit and go down the stairs. The hall splits: one
   way continues southwest, the other goes down stairs to the northwest.
3. **Southwest to the lever.** Continue southwest, avoiding the pressure plate in the
   middle of the hall (it fires a fire-arrow trap). This leads to Room 2, a small square
   chamber with the boss door lever straight ahead on the southwest wall. Two Imps crouch
   on either side of the lever and a third hides out of view in the east corner. A
   noble's corpse in the middle holds Root Resin (x5). Pull the lever to open the boss
   door.
4. **Northwest to the boss door.** Go back to the split and take the stairs northwest.
   Avoid the second pressure plate in the middle of the hall, just before the room (another
   fire-arrow trap). Room 3 holds the boss door and three Imps: two crouched on either
   side of the door and one hanging directly above the room's entrance, on the southeast
   wall. A Ghost Glovewort [1] is in this room.
5. **Boss.** Go through the boss door and the fog gate.

## Enemies

- **Imp** x9 — 196 HP, 67 runes, stance 30. Can be backstabbed. One guard counter from a
  straight sword breaks their stance. They resist most damage types; strike damage works
  best. See [Imp].
- **Fire-arrow traps** — two pressure plates, one in each hall after the split.

## Boss

**Grave Warden Duelist** — 1,095 HP, 1,700 runes, stance 65. Takes extra slash damage
(+10%). Many of his attacks leave openings for a backstab, and he can also be parried and
stance-broken.

## Items

Pickups (one time only):

- **Grave Glovewort [1]** — two in Room 1
- **Root Resin** (x5) — on the noble's corpse in the lever room
- **Ghost Glovewort [1]** — in the boss door room

Boss drop:

- **Banished Knight Engvall** — spirit ashes

## Tips

- Pull the lever first, or you'll reach the boss door and find it shut.
- Step around the pressure plates in the middle of each hall.
- Imps hang from walls and ceilings above doorways. Look up before walking in.
