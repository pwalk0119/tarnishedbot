## Overview

The windswept hills between Limgrave and Stormveil Castle. Margit, the Fell Omen guards
the castle's front gate, so most players come here once they're ready to take on the
castle, around level 20-30. [check]

## Getting There

Take the road north from Gatefront Ruins through Stormgate. To avoid Stormgate's soldiers
and ballista, ride up the cliff path to the west via Stormhill Shack. [check]

## Sites of Grace

- Stormhill Shack — the small shack on the cliffside path west of Stormgate
- Castleward Tunnel — the tunnel before Margit's arena, at Stormveil's gate
- Margit, the Fell Omen — appears after you defeat Margit
- Warmaster's Shack — a ruined shack in the northern part of Stormhill
- Deathtouched Catacombs — inside the catacombs
- Limgrave Tower Bridge — on the bridge to the Divine Tower of Limgrave
- Divine Tower of Limgrave — at the top of the tower
- Saintsbridge — at the bridge in northern Stormhill [check]

## Points of Interest

- **Stormgate** — the fortified gate on the main road, guarded by soldiers and a ballista.
- **Stormhill Shack** — Roderika waits here. See [Roderika].
- **Warmaster's Shack** — Knight Bernahl sells Ashes of War here. See [Knight Bernahl].
- **Stormhill Evergaol** — a sealed arena holding a Crucible Knight. Needs a Stonesword Key.
- **Deathtouched Catacombs** — short catacomb with a Black Knife Assassin boss.
- **Limgrave Tower Bridge / Divine Tower** — reached later, from inside Stormveil. [check]

## Bosses

- **Margit, the Fell Omen** — at Castleward Tunnel; blocks the way into Stormveil.
- **Crucible Knight** — inside Stormhill Evergaol.
- **Black Knife Assassin** — at the end of Deathtouched Catacombs.

## NPCs

- **Roderika** — at Stormhill Shack. Gives the Chrysalids' Memento. [check]
- **Knight Bernahl** — at Warmaster's Shack; sells Ashes of War.
- **Kalé**, **Blaidd** and others live in Limgrave proper. See their entries.

## Items

- **Stonesword Key** — needed for the evergaol and imp statues; sold by Twin Maiden Husks
  and found around Limgrave. [check]

## Tips

- Margit is a big difficulty jump. Use Spirit Ashes, or summon Sorcerer Rogier from his
  sign outside the arena. [check]
- Using the cliff path via Stormhill Shack lets you skip Stormgate.
- Talk to Roderika and Bernahl before fighting Margit.
